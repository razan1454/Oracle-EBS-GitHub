Step 1: Confirm the user has the job role
    1.Go to Navigator → Tools → Security Console.
    2.Open the Users tab and search for your username.
    3.Check that the required role is listed under the user’s roles. If it isn’t, click Edit, then Add Role, select the role, and save.


Step 2: Open the data access task
    1.Go to Navigator → My Enterprise → Setup and Maintenance.
   2.Search for the task Manage Data Access for Users and open it.


Step 3: Add the data access
    1.Search for your User Name and click Search.
    2.In the results, click + Create (the plus icon).
    3.Fill in the row: 

  -User Name: your user
  -Role: the job or data role from your assignment
  -Security Context: the type, such as Business Unit, Ledger, or Legal Entity
  -Security Context Value: the specific item from your assignment, such as the BU name

4.Add one row for each role and context combination you need.
5.Click Save and Close.
