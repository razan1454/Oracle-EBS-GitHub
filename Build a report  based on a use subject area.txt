1.Open the analysis builder. Go to Navigator → Tools → Reports and Analytics, then click Browse Catalog.

2.Create a new analysis. Click Create → Analysis.

3.Choose the subject area. Select the one that matches your data, such as Payables Invoices - Transactions Real Time, Purchasing - Purchase Orders Real Time, or Workforce Management - Worker Assignment Real Time.

4.Select columns. Expand the folders and double-click the fields you want. Each folder is a dimension (like Supplier or Business Unit) or a fact folder holding the numbers (like Invoice Amount).

5.Add filters. Hover over a column in the Criteria tab, click More Options → Filter, and set conditions such as Invoice Date is in the last 30 days or Status = Validated.

6.Preview the results. Click the Results tab, then sort, rename headings, and add totals as needed.

7.Add views (optional). Click New View to add a chart, pivot table, or graph, then arrange them in the Compound Layout.

8.Add prompts (optional). Click New View → Filters → Prompts → Column Prompt so users can pick values (like date or business unit) when they run the report.

9.Save the report. Click Save, choose My Folders or Shared Folders, and name it.

10.Run and share. Open it from the catalog to run it, then export to Excel, PDF, or CSV from the Export option.
